ACADEMIC RULES - FICTIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY

1. Attendance Requirement
Students must maintain a minimum of 75% attendance in each course during
a semester to be eligible to appear for the end-semester examination.
Students falling between 65% and 75% attendance may apply for
condonation through the department office, subject to a valid medical
or personal reason supported by documentation.

2. Grading System
The institute follows a 10-point grading scale. Grades range from O
(Outstanding, 90-100 marks) down to F (Fail, below 40 marks). A
student's Semester Grade Point Average (SGPA) is calculated as the
credit-weighted average of grade points across all courses registered
in that semester.

3. Credit Requirements
Undergraduate students must complete a minimum of 160 credits over
eight semesters to be eligible for graduation. At least 20 of these
credits must come from open elective courses outside the student's
core department.

4. Re-registration and Backlogs
A student who fails a core course must re-register for that course the
next time it is offered. A maximum of two backlog courses may be
carried forward into the final year; students with more than two
backlogs will not be permitted to register for final-year core courses.

5. Class Conduct
Students are expected to attend classes on time. Repeated late entry
(more than 10 minutes after the scheduled start, three or more times
in a month) may result in the student being marked absent for that
session at the instructor's discretion.

6. Academic Integrity
Any form of plagiarism, unauthorized collaboration during individual
assessments, or impersonation during examinations is treated as a
serious academic integrity violation and is referred to the Academic
Discipline Committee.
