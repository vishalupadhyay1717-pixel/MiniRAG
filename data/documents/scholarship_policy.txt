SCHOLARSHIP POLICY - FICTIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY

1. Merit Scholarship
Students who achieve a Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) of 9.0 or
above at the end of an academic year are eligible for the Merit
Scholarship, which waives 50% of tuition fees for the following
academic year. Eligibility is reassessed annually.

2. Need-Based Financial Aid
Students whose family income is below the threshold defined by the
institute's Financial Aid Committee (currently updated each year) may
apply for need-based financial aid covering up to 75% of tuition fees.
Applications must be submitted with supporting income documentation
within the first month of the academic year.

3. Sports Scholarship
Students who represent the institute at state or national level in a
recognized sport, and who maintain a minimum CGPA of 6.0, are eligible
for a Sports Scholarship covering hostel fees for the academic year in
which the representation occurred.

4. Scholarship Renewal Conditions
All scholarships are renewed annually and are contingent on maintaining
both the required academic performance (CGPA) and a minimum attendance
of 75%, in line with the institute's general academic rules. A
scholarship will be withdrawn if a student is found responsible for an
academic integrity violation.

5. Application Process
Scholarship applications are submitted online through the student
portal. The Scholarship Committee reviews applications within 4 weeks
and communicates the decision via the student's registered institute
email address. Appeals against a rejected application may be filed
within 2 weeks of the decision.

6. Combining Scholarships
A student may hold only one primary scholarship (Merit, Need-Based, or
Sports) at a time. However, a student may additionally apply for
one-time book grants or conference travel grants, which are not
considered primary scholarships and can be combined with any of the
above.
