EXAMINATION POLICY - FICTIONAL INSTITUTE OF TECHNOLOGY

1. Examination Structure
Each course is assessed through a combination of Continuous Assessment
(40% of total marks, covering quizzes, assignments and a mid-semester
test) and an End-Semester Examination (60% of total marks). A student
must score at least 30% in the End-Semester Examination independently,
in addition to an overall passing score, to clear the course.

2. Examination Hall Rules
Students must carry their institute identity card to every examination.
Electronic devices, including smartwatches and mobile phones, are
strictly prohibited inside the examination hall. Students arriving
more than 30 minutes after the start of an examination will not be
permitted entry.

3. Re-evaluation Requests
Students who wish to have their answer script re-evaluated must submit
a written request to the Controller of Examinations within 7 working
days of result declaration, along with the prescribed re-evaluation
fee. Re-evaluation results are final and cannot be further contested.

4. Supplementary Examinations
Students who fail a course in the regular end-semester examination are
eligible to appear for a supplementary examination, typically conducted
within 4-6 weeks of result declaration. A student may attempt a maximum
of three supplementary attempts for a single course.

5. Malpractice During Examinations
Any student found using unauthorized material, communicating with
another candidate, or engaging in impersonation during an examination
will have their examination for that course cancelled immediately and
the matter will be referred to the Examination Malpractice Committee.
A first-time malpractice case may result in cancellation of that one
examination; repeated offenses can lead to suspension for up to one
academic year.

6. Grade Improvement
A student may register to retake a course once, purely for grade
improvement, even after passing it, provided they have not exhausted
their maximum permissible course attempts. Only the higher of the two
grades is considered in the final transcript.
